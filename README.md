About Me
Hello! I'm Kenea Merga Beyene, an Information Technology and Administration professional with over 13 years of professional experience in Information Technology (IT), Data Management, Administrative Support, Digital Systems, and Organizational Operations.
I hold a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science from Wollega University and a Bachelor of Arts in Management from Rift Valley University. My educational background combines technical expertise with management, leadership, organizational administration, and problem-solving skills.
